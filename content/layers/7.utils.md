The utils layer has small helpers that the other layers share: currency formatting and string utilities.

An app does not add it. Core extends it, so every Pluto app has these helpers.

## What it adds

- **Currency formatting**: format a price for a locale and a currency.
- **String utilities**: slugs and other small text helpers.
