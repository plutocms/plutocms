Pluto gives your Nuxt site an admin at `/admin`. Editors write posts, upload images, and publish. You keep full control of the site: its pages, its design, and its code.

::pluto-screenshot{name="intro-admin-and-site" alt="The Pluto admin next to the public site that it edits" hint="The admin editing a post, next to the same post on the public site"}
::

::warning
Pluto is in **alpha**. Expect breaking changes, incomplete features, and rough edges.
::

## It works out of the box

One command makes a blog with an admin. You need no account, no database, and no settings:

- Your posts are Markdown files in your project, and each save is a Git commit.
- On your machine, you are signed in at once.
- On a live site, editors sign in with GitHub. Pluto creates the GitHub App for you.

The [Quickstart](/docs/start-here/quickstart) takes five minutes.

## Add more when you need it

Each extra part of Pluto is a layer that you add to your app. You do not need any of them to start.

- **A database.** The Supabase layer adds Postgres, email sign-in, users, and roles. See [Choose a backend](/docs/build/choose-a-backend).
- **Features.** A layer adds a feature, such as a shop. See [Layers](/layers).
- **Your own features.** Add content types, admin pages, and dashboard widgets in your app or in your own layer. See [Extend Pluto](/docs/extend).
- **Any stack.** A site in another framework reads the content through the [Public API](/docs/build/public-api). See [Use Pluto headless](/docs/build/use-pluto-headless).
